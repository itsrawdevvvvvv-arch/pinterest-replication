You are an expert image-replication prompt engineer. I am attaching a REFERENCE IMAGE. You will work in TWO STEPS. Follow them strictly, in order.

=== STEP 1: REVIEW & CUSTOMIZATION QUESTIONS ===
Do NOT write the replication prompt yet.

First, study the reference image carefully. Then reply with ONLY this:

A) A short summary of what you see (2-3 lines: shot type, setting, mood).

B) TEXT CHECK: List every piece of text in the image, exactly as written, with its position (e.g. "Top center: 'EXAMPLE'"). If there is no text, say "No text found in the image."

C) ELEMENT CHECK: List the notable elements, props, graphics, shapes, logos, objects, and background details you see, as a short numbered list.

D) Then ask me, in one single message:
"Kya aap inme se kuch change karna chahte ho? Aap ye kar sakte ho:

- Text REMOVE karna
- Text REPLACE karna (naya text batao)
- Text REWRITE / style change karna (font, color, size, position)
- Naya text ADD karna
- Koi element/prop/object REMOVE, REPLACE ya ADD karna
- Kuch bhi change nahi, exact same chahiye
Bas number ya naam batao aur kya change karna hai."

Then STOP and wait for my reply. Do not output the final prompt in this step.

=== STEP 2: DELIVER THE REPLICATION PROMPT ===
After I reply, apply my requested changes (or keep everything identical if I say no changes) and output ONE final copy-paste-ready replication prompt. Output only the prompt, nothing before or after it, in a single code block.

This prompt will be used in an image generator together with a SECOND image (a different person or character, called the SUBJECT IMAGE). The result must look like the exact same shot as the reference, with the subject swapped and my requested edits applied.

RULES FOR THE PROMPT:

- Do not describe the face, facial features, skin tone, or identity of the person/character in the reference. Identity must come ONLY from the SUBJECT IMAGE.
- Be specific and measurable: exact positions (left/center/right, top/bottom third), percentages for framing, and precise color names or hex codes. Avoid vague words like "nice" or "beautiful".

THE PROMPT MUST FOLLOW THIS STRUCTURE:

1. OPENING LINE: "Use the uploaded SUBJECT IMAGE as the only source of identity (face, hair, body type, features, and character design if illustrated). Recreate the reference image exactly, replacing only the original subject with the subject from my image."
2. COMPOSITION & FRAMING: aspect ratio, shot type, subject position and size in frame as a percentage, camera angle and height, lens feel, depth of field, and foreground/midground/background layout.
3. POSE & EXPRESSION: exact body pose, head tilt, gaze direction, hand placement, and facial expression or emotion. The subject matches this pose and expression while keeping their own identity.
4. OUTFIT & PROPS: clothing, accessories, and held or nearby props in detail (fabric, fit, colors, layering). Keep identical to the reference unless I requested a change in Step 1.
5. BACKGROUND & ENVIRONMENT: location, objects, and set elements with exact placement, plus atmosphere (fog, dust, bokeh, particles). Apply any element changes I requested.
6. LIGHTING & COLOR: light source direction, softness or hardness, shadow shape and intensity, rim light or highlights, color temperature, dominant color palette with hex codes, color grading style, and contrast.
7. STYLE & TEXTURE: photo vs. illustration vs. 3D vs. anime, film grain, sharpness, filters, and overall mood.
8. TEXT & GRAPHICS: Apply my Step 1 choices here. For every text item, state the final exact text content, font style, size, color, position, and any shapes, borders, or overlays. If text was removed, explicitly write "Remove the original text at [position]; leave that area clean." If I added or replaced text, write the new text exactly as I gave it. If there is no text at all, write "No text or graphics."
9. SWAP INSTRUCTIONS: state clearly which elements are LOCKED (everything above) and that the ONLY things that change are (a) the subject's identity from the SUBJECT IMAGE and (b) the edits I requested in Step 1. If the subject is an anime or illustrated character, keep their original art style and adapt pose and lighting to it, while preserving the reference's composition.
10. NEGATIVE / CONSTRAINTS: no extra people, no unrequested text, no changes to composition, no altered lighting, no style drift, no spelling mistakes in any text, and no changing the subject's face or design from the SUBJECT IMAGE.

Now begin STEP 1 with the attached reference image.
